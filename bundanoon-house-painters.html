Do You Feel Your Home Is Starting To Look Tired Or Needing A Refresh?

Bundanoon is a beautiful Southern Highlands village and amongst the most popular and desired. There are many examples in Bundanoon of beautifully painted homes and if you’re looking to sell they are currently achieving huge sale prices.

If your home is fading, flaking and tired it might be time to consider repainting your home.

Why Not Just Paint Your House Yourself?

You could certainly repaint for house exterior yourself, it’s a great challenge and when completed is a job well done.

When building a house, the painting is the final step and one of the most important, if it’s done badly then even the best masterbuilt home will look average. A quality paint project will ensure that any possible imperfections in works are minimised and that the house looks fantastic.

As experienced painters we can assist with the whole process from start to finish. Appropriate colours, swatches, type of paint, style guides, preparing the house including removing flaking paint, sanding surfaces, repairing holes and other wear and then applying the new finish.
