Southern Highlands Painting Solutions by Locals

Welcome to one of the Southern Highland’s leading painting companies, Dan Eaton painting. We’re a family-run business with over 25 year’s local experience in all areas of painting, providing excellent service at a cost effective price for our dear customers.

Our services include the residential painting of homes, both interior and exterior, floor painting, roof sealing and painting and commercial and industrial paint solutions.

Our primary service areas are in the Southern Highlands towns and villages of Bowral, Moss Vale, Exeter, Bundanoon and Penrose.

Do you have an out of date, tired or newly renovated home that needs a fresh coat of paint? Our team are experienced with various techniques, styles, finishes and colour schemes that will help bring your home to life.
